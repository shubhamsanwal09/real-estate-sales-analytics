Real Estate Sales Analytics

An Excel-based model for analysing residential sales, inventory, customers and revenue.

This project simulates the sales operations of a residential real estate developer. The workbook connects unit-level inventory, customer information, booking data, payment tracking and management reporting in one Excel model.

What the model covers

  • Residential inventory and booking status
  • Customer and buyer profile analysis
  • Booking and payment tracking
  • Revenue and sales performance
  • Customer segmentation and payment risk
  • Management KPIs and executive dashboard

Workbook structure

The workbook contains Inventory Master, Customer Master, Payment Tracker, Sales Summary and Dashboard Visuals sheets. Each sheet represents a specific area of the residential sales operation and supports structured analysis and management reporting.
